The Five-Agent Engine

Five Agents. One Platform. Every Regulated Workflow.

Five specialized AI agents work as a coordinated team — planning, executing, auditing, learning, and negotiating on your behalf. All on a single platform that speaks your industry's language.

1

The Planner

Understands what needs to happen before anything moves.

Ingests your operational objective and maps the exact sequence of actions needed, accounting for dependencies, regulatory requirements, and failure modes before execution.

2

The Executor

Does the work — across browsers, APIs, and systems simultaneously.

Operates in both browser environments and structured APIs. 24/7 speed, without the error accumulation of manual work.

3

The Auditor

Creates the paper trail regulators expect.

Logs every action, decision, and outcome in a format suitable for regulatory examination, SOC 2 audit, or internal review — automatically.

4

The Learner

Gets smarter without requiring your engineering team.

Analyzes outcomes and feedback signals to continuously improve agent behavior. No retraining cycles. No MLOps team required.

5

The Negotiator

Knows when to involve a human — and makes the handoff seamless.

Manages escalations and edge cases, packages the context, routes to the right person, and tracks resolution to inform future automation.
